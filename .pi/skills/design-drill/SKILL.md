---
name: design-drill
description: Stress-test a plan or design through targeted questioning and consistency checks until the major decisions are clear. Use when Codex should expose weak assumptions and drive the discussion toward a coherent decision.
---

Start with constraints, success criteria, and the decisions that shape the rest of the tree. Use direct research when codebase or project files can answer a question.

Ask one question at a time. Use plain, concrete language the user can answer directly. Do not ask filler questions. Skip only what is trivial or already grounded by evidence.

Relentlessly challenge assumptions, dependencies, tradeoffs, edge cases, failure modes, and hidden coupling.

Each question includes a recommended answer and brief rationale. Avoid vague or overloaded wording. When evidence already points to the best path, say so plainly, and call out unresolved risks.

Finish when the major decisions are clear and no important branch remains unresolved.
